### Evidence trust boundaries

The Owner owns and directly reaps Anchor / Controller / Canary; Controller alone is Worker parent and waiter. The research-only private pipes prevent Worker from writing trusted Owner control messages through stdout, but this does **not** prove a production-grade authenticated IPC transport or arbitrary process-tree lifecycle.

T08 distinguishes **the actual synthetic cleanup observation** (all test-owned processes returned and Owner's direct children were successfully reaped) from **the evidence qualification** (Owner has no legal Worker exit status; `cleanup=UNRESOLVED`). `C3B02_AFTER_WAIT_BEFORE_FINAL` is a controlled fault **timing marker**, not an alternate Worker status channel. A separate future before-Wait Controller crash would require its own contract and could leave arbitrary descendants unobserved; this study does not claim otherwise.

Two user-space admission checks precede native group TERM: live held Anchor / Controller native identity and monotonically bounded `term_admission_cutoff` plus `outer_deadline`. `term_attempts` increments before the signal syscall. There remains an unavoidable **non-atomic window between user-space eligibility and kernel `kill`**, and **no real PID/PGID numeric reuse** is reproduced. The one Canary shows *this test-owned independent group* survived, not the safety of arbitrary other host groups.

Owner and Controller explicitly ignore SIGPIPE so a broken control pipe returns a detectable write error rather than terminating those processes by default. T08 is a deliberate Controller final-result omission **after** Worker wait; a full injected EPIPE failure-path suite and arbitrary FD leak audit are not yet proven. Synthetic Worker/Anchor/Controller self-expiry bounds and private gate cleanup were observed for these modes, but cannot substitute for generic subtree cleanup, stdout/stderr drain, exit ownership at scale or real Gateway shutdown.

### P2-01 — T05 genuinely exercises the signal admission gate

The native T05 now first proves Worker joined the newly created Anchor PGID and has actually reached ARMED **before** Anchor is cooperatively retired and directly reaped by its legal parent. It then executes `send_term_to_owned_group()` and verifies `anchor_loss_gate_checked=1`, `after_loss_attempts=0`, `term_attempts=0` and `term_succeeded=0`. The sole Controller legitimately reaps the Worker after a cooperative control byte; direct children and required owned descriptors close within the single original deadline. T05 does **not** pretend that a cached numeric PGID alone is authorization.

**Independent test-build mutation:** `C3B02_MUT_SKIP_ANCHOR_CHECK` is only compile-eligible when paired with `C3B02_TEST_STALE_KILL_STUB`. In the T05 fixed mode, after the deleted Anchor gate would have authorized a group signal, the call is **intercepted before any real kernel `kill(-PGID,...)`** and reports a *simulated* ESRCH with `after_loss_attempts=1`, `term_attempts=1`, `native_signals=0`. This **cannot** establish a kernel-atomic check→kill credential or prove safety under real PGID reuse.

### P2-02 — close() results must precede VERIFIED

`close_fd()` performs a real `close()` exactly once, records nonzero return (including ambiguous EINTR) without retrying a possibly reused FD, and propagates failure to per-process `managed_fd_close_ok`. `close_child()` returns an aggregated status after attempting *both* owned ends. Owner's controlled pipe ends and root dir close now occur **before** calculating `owner_fds_closed` and `cleanup_complete`. Controller closes Worker managed pipes and includes `worker_fds_closed` in its legally sourced Final; Owner may only accept this as `controller_worker_fds_closed=1` after validating the complete Controller Final. All required trusted observations, direct-child reaps, FD closes and the original `outer_deadline` must succeed before `cleanup_complete=1`.

**Native failure injections (separate temporary C builds, newly owned test FD only):** `C3B02_FAULT_OWNER_CLOSE` and `C3B02_FAULT_CONTROLLER_CLOSE` first actually close the managed FD, then inject a synthetic EIO result into the checked close path. Their exact expected fault records show the failed Owner or Controller FD axis and `cleanup_complete=0`, with `C3B02_BLOCK phase=fd_close`; neither can produce a positive native success record. These tests prove **the implemented handling of simulated return failures**, not that all real Darwin kernel close-error behaviors have occurred.

In the two original omit-SIGDEF and omit-SIGMASK *mutants*, Owner rejects the unsafe Worker signal state before opening the execution gate and never receives an authenticated Controller Final; under the stricter cleanup contract these mutant paths now truthfully record `cleanup_complete=0/UNRESOLVED` rather than asserting VERIFIED on an unobserved Worker-FD close. The original independent mutation checks remain positive for detection, with no real group signal attempt. That intentional evidence downgrade does **not** affect T01/T02/T03 native positive and negative executions.
